# The Blessed
https://hearthfire.camp/game/ThPTrnJaqcaPVYg6UcjL/blessed

A human raised in the Great Wood. Her parents lived among the Forest Folk.

She doesn't have the distinctive eyes of those with Forest Folk ancestry, but there's a shifting quality to her hazel eyes that still makes people talk.

Feels a strong connection to the earth, and often goes barefoot.

Gifted with the knack for talking with beasts and spirits. *Do villagers believe this is the blessing of Danu, or a Forest Folk craft she learned the trick of?*

The children of Stonetop like her better than the adults, many of whom find her strange, if not spooky. *Deep water is dangerous. Everyone knows this.*

Gives tribute at the shrine of Danu.
## Relations

### Village
River lives at the edge of town, where she keeps goats. She contributes milk and cheese as well as meat and pelts. *from hunting?*

Hunters from Stonetop seek her guidance when headed into the Wood.
### Family
River's parents were humans living among the Forest Folk. One stormy night when she was a child, they went out and never returned.

Bramble, Moss, and Pebble are her mastiffs.

### Other
River remembers [[Shep Marlowe|Shep]] being kind to her when she arrived in Stonetop, seemingly an orphan. She was the first there for him when he returned from his violent exploits.

Crosses paths with [[Aurelia Senka]] when they both wander in the vicinity of Stonetop.

Provides [[Steadfast of the Ackord|Steadfast]] with certain animal goods specifically for rites of Aratis.

Left food for [[Lenox Fyle|Lenox]] and [[The Grateful]] while they were in the midst of [[Otash|otash]] withdrawals.
