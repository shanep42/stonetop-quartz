# The Marshal
https://hearthfire.camp/game/ThPTrnJaqcaPVYg6UcjL/marshal

## Description
Came with [[The Grateful]] from [[Lygos]], where they were a gang of [[Otash|otash]] addicts. The ripped off a dealer's stash and fled to the edge of the world, and Stonetop.

They tried to sell otash in Stonetop but the people were poorer and their supply more finite than they counted on, and they used most of it themselves.

The villagers, even Judge [[Magistrate Ackord]], forgave rather than punished, and even helped the band recover through withdrawals, earning their intense gratitude.

When [[Brennan|Brennan and the Claws]] menaced Stonetop, maiming most of its informal militia as a warning, Lenox and the Grateful dealt with the threat by poisoning most of the Claws. Brennan escaped.

## Relations
### Village
The Grateful stepped into the role of a standing militia for Stonetop following the incident with the Claws.

They are not trained soldiers but are street-smart and scrappy enough to break up fights and discourage conflict from getting out of hand.

### Other

Leader of [[The Grateful]], a band of penitent smugglers from Lygos turned Stonetop militia.

[[River|River]] left food for the Lenox and the Grateful when the citizens of Stonetop nursed them through withdrawals.
