# The Lightbearer
https://hearthfire.camp/game/ThPTrnJaqcaPVYg6UcjL/lightbearer

## Description
Her mother [[Golenthi Senka (Dead)|Golenthi]] was a famous Lightbearer that died battling a [[The River King (Banished)|demon]] near Marshedge.

Born in Marshedge, brought back to Stonetop, her mother's hometown, following Golenthi's death. 

Goes with her eyes covered, and sometimes needs assistance around town, but wanders often in the Great Wood, sometimes for days at a time.

Goat's milk soap is her favorite.

## Relations

### Village
As Lightbearer, she serves a spiritual role, especially for Helior worshippers. A small contingent of her mother's followers came with her as a baby to Stonetop. *Are they still here?*
Her religious role include serving as a confessor.
Her ability to abjure spirits might send villagers to her seeking "exorcisms."

### Family
Raised by [[Hethenar]]
Daughter to [[Golenthi Senka (Dead)]]

### Other
Crosses paths with [[River]] when they both wander the vicinity of Stonetop, and trade for milk from River's goats.
