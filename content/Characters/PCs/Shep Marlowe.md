# The Heavy
https://hearthfire.camp/game/ThPTrnJaqcaPVYg6UcjL/heavy

## Description

Good-hearted, but a determination to help led to a habit of getting involved in other people's business, which led to a habit of getting in fights.

Left Stonetop for many years, possibly after a particular incident of violence went too far. Stories of him from the surrounding area filtered back in his absence. *How old was he when he left? How long was he gone?*

Continued to attract more trouble and violence in his time away, seemingly more than even he could stomach. Seems to view his return to Stonetop and the hard but peaceful work at the forge a sort of retirement.

His dreams are haunted by his past, and things darker still.

The dreams worsened after he was struck by lightning, which villagers see as an obvious sign from Tor, and only increase the rumor swirling around him.

## Notes
Everyone loves to talk about the time he was struck by lightning, and woke up covered in glowing runic marks.

They're less keen to discuss what happened to [[Urbgen (Dead)|Urbgen]], even if he did have it coming.

Refers to his time exploits away from Stonetop as "The War." *Euphemistically? Diffusing with humor?*


## Relations

### Village
Shep is the smith for Stonetop. The village relies upon his forge for all their metalwork, a huge responsibility. He buries himself in the work, and sometimes forgets to eat.

In his youth, he was always willing to step in to break up (or at least finish) a fight or send off a ruffian. *Does he abstain from that kind of thing, now that Stonetop has a dedicated militia?*
