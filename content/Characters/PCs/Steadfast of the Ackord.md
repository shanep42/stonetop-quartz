# The Judge
https://hearthfire.camp/game/ThPTrnJaqcaPVYg6UcjL/judge

## Description
Called Steady informally (she prefers formality)

From Clan Ackord. They are one of Stonetop's oldest families, believed to have been among its founders. They also have a long tradition in the priesthood of Aratis the Law-Giver.

40 years old. Imposing. Red hair starting to go silver.

Has made a lifelong special project of studying the Old Wall, and organizing the reclamation of its stone for new village structures.

Trained as an acolyte since age 14, inspired by her father and uncle.
### Relationships

### Village
Newly ordained as a full Judge, Steadfast is empowered to settle disputes among villagers that they cannot settle themselves.

Judges are also historians of their jurisdiction, and Steady helps keep the Chronicle.
