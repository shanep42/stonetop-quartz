Leader of the Claws, the bandits that loom largest in Stonetop's recent memory.

The Claws would shake down villagers when they caught them travelling, but Stonetop is largely too poor to bother with. They'd outright rob travelling traders, but to Stonetop they were more bullies than bandits.

Their aggression escalated partly because of the arrival of [[Lenox Fyle|Lenox]] and [[The Grateful]]. The Claws got wind that Stonetop was seeing a slight influx of foreigners, including rumors of fugitives from Lygos carrying [[Otash|something valuable]].

The Claws maimed most of Stonetop's militia to show they meant business.

Lenox and the Grateful dealt with the threat to their new home by poisoning the Claws.

Brennan escaped, and is rumored to be recruiting, maybe putting together new Claws.
