The River King was a demon that lurked beneath the Dread River and plagued the town of Marshedge before being defeated by [[Golenthi Senka (Dead)|Golenthi Senka]], who perished in the attempt.

Few outside the church of Helior know much of it. 

If someone in Stonetop has ever heard of the River King, it's probably because the overheard name made a useful boogeyman: "Stay away from deep water, or the River King will throw you in his still and boil you down for his whiskey."
