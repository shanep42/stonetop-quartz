A drug made from otari, a southern plant with a pleasant stimulant effect, traditionally made into refreshing tea. 

Otari grows most commonly in desert oases and cannot normally grow in Stonetop's more northern climbs. It grows tall like bamboo but in flat green blades, looking almost like kelp.

Otash is a waxy oil that can be refined from otari by baking at incredibly high heat. Otash wax can be smoked or eaten, though it's often tinctured into strong alcohol to be more palatable (probably most popular method among Stonetop customers, plus it stretches the supply).

Otash causes manic euphoria, numbness to pain, loss of impulse control. Long term usage causes mood swings, paranoia, and shakes.
